# Minimum Evidence Requirements

> Normative chi tiết theo Rule ID/target nằm tại
> `rule-to-evidence-requirement-matrix.md`.

Every evaluation requires:

1. report/target association;
2. immutable target identity/state/content snapshot;
3. candidate Rule ID/version;
4. rule-specific observation;
5. provenance/freshness/availability;
6. counter-evidence and missing-evidence assessment;
7. target/action applicability.

`RESOLVE/REJECT` requires `SUFFICIENT` and no unresolved critical conflict. Pattern, media,
jurisdiction, protected class, IP authority or privacy consent requirements cannot be waived by
high model likelihood.

Structural completeness không đồng nghĩa rule sufficiency. `REJECT` còn yêu cầu complete evaluated
scope; missing critical evidence phải route manual.
