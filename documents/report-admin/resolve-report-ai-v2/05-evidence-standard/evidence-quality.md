# Evidence Quality

| Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| `HIGH` | authoritative source, direct observation, intact provenance/freshness |
| `MEDIUM` | reliable but indirect/partial |
| `LOW` | weak provenance, stale, ambiguous or heavily transformed |
| `UNUSABLE` | unavailable, unverifiable, corrupted or outside authority |

Quality is per evidence item. A high-quality item may still be insufficient for a rule requiring
pattern, context, jurisdiction or claimant authority.
