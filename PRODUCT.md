# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CafeStory users browse cafe stories and discussions in a mixed social feed. Cafe owners use the same product surface to promote an active cafe page with a paid sponsored placement.

## Product Purpose

CafeStory helps people discover cafes through community posts while giving cafe owners a clear, trustworthy way to reach relevant readers. Sponsored placements should fit naturally into the feed without being mistaken for organic posts.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, and tidy. The interface should feel calm and familiar, using the existing CafeStory visual language while making sponsored content clearly labeled.

## Anti-references

Do not make sponsored content impersonate an organic blog post. Avoid fake social actions, excessive advertising decoration, forced repeated cards, and layouts that create unexplained empty space or horizontal overflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep sponsored content visually consistent with the feed container.
- Make the sponsored nature and destination action explicit.
- Prefer predictable responsive geometry over decorative variation.
- Preserve content hierarchy and readable touch targets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use basic WCAG-oriented checks: readable contrast, meaningful image and control labels, keyboard focus on Web, and sufficiently large touch targets on Mobile. Tracking failures must not block the destination action.
